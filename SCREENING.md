# Screening — One-Star Review of Something Essential

Mission: `u-0xb3e689-one-star-review-of-something-essential-c7234629` (pool $5, top 5 split $1 each).
Brief (judge criteria in order): (1) one-star review of an everyday essential, deadpan, unmistakably about the thing itself; (2) sharpest true grievance, the small daily annoyance everyone recognises but never seen written down; (3) holds the deadpan: one star, no winking, no explaining, no exclamation marks; (4) turns in the last few words; (5) tight and specific, one joke told cleanly, no filler, no second joke.
Winner rules (26 Nov study): 3 beats; final clause alone is the funniest part and reframes; ≥2 concrete specifics (digit / proper name / model-part id); human stakes in one phrase; target 120–160 chars, never under ~100; form fidelity (reads as a real one-star review). Avoid crowded targets (sun / Mondays / gravity) unless genuinely distinct.
All candidates composed from the brief only. No other make.fun entry was read. All ASCII, no exclamation marks. Char counts verified by script.

Scoring: each criterion 0–5, in brief order. Total /25.
